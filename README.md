## About the Project

Ecomorphis is an innovative waste management platform designed to connect citizens, municipalities, and waste processors for sustainable solutions. It addresses critical challenges such as inadequate source segregation, elevated operational costs, and low public awareness. The platform provides waste tracking, real-time monitoring, and community engagement tools to help build a greener future.

## Key Features & Solutions

* **IoT-Enabled Smart Bins:** Automatically sort waste to improve source segregation directly at the point of disposal.


* **Cloud Dashboard & Mobile App:** Delivers a real-time monitoring and reporting platform to track participation and enhance awareness.


* **GPS Route Optimization:** Reduces operational costs by utilizing GPS technology to optimize waste collection routes for municipal fleets.


* **Gamified "Seed to Tree" Model:** An incentive-based system that turns user contributions into visual trees, rewarding proper waste segregation and encouraging ongoing participation.


* **Community-Driven Learning:** Interactive educational modules that combine waste management training with rewards to foster lasting behavioral change.


* **Data Accuracy:** Utilizes geo-tagged images to verify waste disposal practices and ensure system accountability.



## Tech Stack

* **Programming Language:** Python 3.x for core scripting, logic, and integrations.


* **Frontend/UI:** Streamlit for widgets and layout, alongside Custom CSS/HTML for eco-themed styling using Google Fonts.


* **Backend/Logic:** Python 3.x utilizing Session State to manage user data, complaints, reward points, and inventory.


* **Data:** Pandas for in-memory storage, filtering, and facility DataFrames.


* **Media & Geolocation:** ImgBB, YouTube, and Placeholder.com for media integration; `st.map` for geolocation features.


* **Deployment:** Supported on Streamlit Cloud and local execution environments.



## Implementation Methodology

1. **Phase 1 - Research & Planning:** Conduct surveys and define specifications for the app and smart bins.


2. **Phase 2 - Prototyping:** Develop the gamified mobile application.


3. **Phase 3 - Integration:** Connect the mobile app with the centralized cloud dashboard.


4. **Phase 4 - Pilot Testing:** Deploy the solution in a small community to collect operational data.


5. **Phase 5 - Feedback:** Analyze system performance and improve existing features based on pilot results.


6. **Phase 6 - Full Deployment & Scaling:** Expand to larger areas and establish partnerships with NGOs.



## Business Model

* **B2G (Business-to-Government):** Subscription or annual contracts providing services to municipal bodies.


* **B2B (Business-to-Business):** Smart bin and dashboard licensing tailored for private companies and campuses.


* **Freemium Citizen App:** A free application for citizens that offers premium features for communities.


* **Data Insights:** Revenue generated by selling waste analytics and reports to governments for urban planning purposes.



## Impact

* **Environmental:** Contributes to cleaner and greener cities by reducing landfill waste and lowering carbon emissions.


* **Economic:** Yields a 30-40% reduction in fuel and manpower costs while generating revenue from recyclables like plastic and metal.


* **Social & Public Health:** Promotes active citizen participation, improves community hygiene, and reduces open dumping and foul odors.
